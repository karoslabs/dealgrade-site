What actually forces a sale in Dubai

  • A lender enforcing

    Under Law No. 14 of 2008 a defaulting borrower is served through the Land Department, and after the cure period the unit can go to court-ordered auction. Sellers come here before that happens, because a private sale beats a hammer price.

  • An off-plan payment plan

    The buyer cannot fund the next milestone, and the developer can terminate and retain part of what has been paid. It transfers as an assignment of the sale agreement, with the developer's consent and fee, and not as an ordinary sale.

  • Service charge arrears

    Whatever is outstanding to the owners' association is settled at transfer. Confirm the figure before you agree a price. A discount that exactly matches the arrears is not a discount.

Where a mortgage is still on the title, the transfer needs the lender's liability letter, a no-objection certificate and a blocking payment at the trustee office. That is what turns a four-week close into a six-week close, and it is flagged on every card where it applies.
