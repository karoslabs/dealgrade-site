What forces a sale in Dubai, in plain terms.

  • A lender enforcing

    Under Law No. 14 of 2008 a defaulting borrower is served through the Land Department, and after the cure period the unit can go to court-ordered auction.

  • An off-plan payment plan

    If the buyer cannot fund the next milestone, the developer can terminate and retain part of what has been paid. The home then transfers as an assignment of the sale agreement, with the developer's consent and fee.

  • Service charge arrears

    Whatever is outstanding to the owners' association is settled at transfer. Confirm the figure before you agree a price.

Where a mortgage is still on the title, the transfer needs the lender's liability letter, a no-objection certificate and a blocking payment at the trustee office.
