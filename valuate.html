Three steps take you from a home to a grade.

1

Describe the home

Its area, size and bedrooms. The building name, if you know it, makes the biggest difference.

2

See what it is worth

An estimate with a likely range, worked out from registered sales of similar homes.

3

Grade a price

Add the asking price and get a grade. A is well below what such homes sell for, and F is well above.

The grade says where an asking price sits against real sales.

It compares the asking price with the estimate and allows for how accurate that estimate can be. A small gap on a precise estimate counts for more than the same gap on a rough one.

AWell below market value
BBelow market value

DAbove market value

Every grade comes with the estimate, a likely range, the sales it is based on and a margin of error. An estimate from registered sales, not a certified valuation.
